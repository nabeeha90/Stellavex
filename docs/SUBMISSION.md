# Stellavex: Submission notes

Team Nova Stellavex, Karachi. NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026, Space Mission Design Game.

## Theme fit: The Next Frontier
The 2026 theme advances human exploration of the Moon, Mars and beyond (NASA Space Apps). Stellavex is a frontier
progression: Moon, Mars, an asteroid, then Europa, an ocean world where sunlight is faint, radiation is harsh and
radio links are slow. Each step forces new engineering choices (power, antennas, shielding), and a Frontier Rank
shows how far the player has gone.

## Short description
Stellavex is a free browser game where you design a rocket, spend a fixed budget, and fly it to the Moon,
Mars, or an asteroid. Real physics and NASA data decide every outcome, random hazards test your design,
and each success or failure is explained with the science behind it.

## Challenge requirements and where we meet them
Mission objectives (3 missions), spacecraft design (craft, power, battery, antenna), scientific instruments (8),
launch vehicles (3), budget, power (solar or nuclear, battery, draw of every part), mass, communications
(antenna, distance, data rate), orbital constraints (delta-v, route, launch window, arrival orbit), a complete
simulation with decisions during flight, and evaluation (score breakdown, build comparison, NASA facts).

## What to show (90-second demo)
1. Home page: the huge rocket rises (0:00).
2. Pick the Moon tutorial, Intermediate difficulty, lab upgrades, build a rocket, pick solar panels and an antenna, tap Exploded view (0:10).
3. Move the fuel slider and point at the delta-v meter (0:25).
4. Launch: countdown, booster separation, flight, a hazard with its alarm and a decision from mission control, landing, NASA facts in the results (0:35).
5. Worlds and NASA resources pages: carousel and sliders (1:10).
6. Mars unlocked banner (1:25).

## Judging themes
Science and physics (rocket equation, delta-v), data (NASA fact sheets), technology (3D browser game,
Python verified backend, five languages), storytelling (named mission control crew, mission stories),
global connection (any browser, daily challenge), art and technology (everything generated in code),
local impact (built in Karachi for students).
